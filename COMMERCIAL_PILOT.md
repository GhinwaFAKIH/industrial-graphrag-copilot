# Industrial GraphRAG Copilot — Evaluation Pilot

## Intended customer
Industrial SMEs, maintenance engineering teams, and industrial software integrators who need to search and connect maintenance documentation and equipment information.

## Problem
Maintenance information is often scattered across documents and disconnected equipment records. Finding relevant evidence can take time.

## Proposed pilot (indicative price: €500, subject to scoping)
- Discovery call to define one narrow investigation workflow.
- Demonstration with synthetic or explicitly approved, non-sensitive customer data.
- Up to five representative equipment records and a small approved document set.
- Evidence-linked answers and an explanation of knowledge-graph relationships.
- A short evaluation summary covering answer relevance, citation quality, limitations and next steps.

## Not included
Live machine control, fault prediction guarantees, safety decisions, production integration, enterprise security certification, or unrestricted ingestion of confidential data.

## Success criteria to agree with buyer
1. Select 10 representative maintenance questions before testing.
2. Evaluate whether cited evidence supports each answer.
3. Record incorrect, unsupported and unanswered responses.
4. Collect user feedback on investigation usefulness.

## Customer discovery questions
- How do engineers currently locate maintenance records and manuals?
- Which investigation takes the most time?
- Which sources would the pilot need to search?
- Who approves new engineering software and what security review is required?
- Would you pay for a narrowly scoped evidence-search pilot if it meets agreed criteria?

## Positioning
This is an evaluation of explainable maintenance information retrieval, not a validated predictive-maintenance or industrial diagnostic product. All public demo data is synthetic.
