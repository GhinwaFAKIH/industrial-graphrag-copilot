
Cooling System Maintenance Guide — Synthetic Demonstration Document

Document ID: MANUAL-COOLING-001

Elevated equipment temperature can be associated with restricted airflow, cooling fan malfunction, or obstructed ventilation.

Recommended inspection procedure:
1. Review temperature measurements.
2. Inspect cooling fan operation.
3. Check airflow paths and ventilation openings.
4. Inspect for accumulated dust or obstruction.
5. Escalate to a qualified maintenance technician before performing any physical intervention.

Temperature elevation alone does not confirm cooling system failure.
