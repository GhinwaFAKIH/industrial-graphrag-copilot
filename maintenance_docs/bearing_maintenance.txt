
Bearing Maintenance Guide — Synthetic Demonstration Document

Document ID: MANUAL-BEARING-001

Excessive vibration and elevated operating temperature may be associated with bearing wear, insufficient lubrication, or mechanical misalignment.

Recommended inspection procedure:
1. Verify operating conditions and review sensor measurements.
2. Inspect the bearing lubrication condition.
3. Check bearing alignment and mechanical mounting.
4. Inspect for visible wear or damage.
5. Escalate to a qualified maintenance technician before performing any physical intervention.

These symptoms are not sufficient to confirm bearing failure. Further inspection is required.
