What the prototype demonstrates

Equipment context

Navigate simulated machine readings and relationships between equipment, components and possible faults.

Hybrid retrieval

Combine RDF/SPARQL knowledge-graph evidence with semantic search over maintenance documents.

Traceable answers

Explore source-linked evidence. The public demo offers deterministic RDF/SPARQL retrieval; the local prototype also includes LLM-assisted answers.

Example investigation

Question: “Why might machine M102 require inspection?”

Prototype workflow: retrieve illustrative sensor readings, inspect graph-linked maintenance observations, find relevant manual excerpts, and produce a cited explanation. The result is not a confirmed diagnosis.
